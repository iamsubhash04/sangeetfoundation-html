Donation FAQs

What is Gift Aid?

If you are a UK taxpayer, Gift Aid lets us claim an extra 25p for every £1 you give, at no cost to you.

Can I donate by bank transfer?

Yes. Use the account details shown on this page and add your name as the payment reference.

Is my donation tax deductible?

Sangeet Global Ltd. is a registered charity (no. 1195762). Please check with your tax adviser about relief that may apply to you.

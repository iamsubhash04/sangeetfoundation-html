Music helps people talk about what is hard to say

Sangeet Foundation is a UK charity. We bring people together through concerts, workshops and online programmes, and point anyone who needs help to the right support.

Raise awareness

Talks and workshops with experts on topics such as autism and ADHD, open to parents, carers and anyone who wants to learn.

Build a network

A community for South Asian families in the UK, online and in person, where no one is left on their own.

Heal through music

Concerts and arts-based sessions that show how music and the arts can support mental health.

Join us at our next event

Past events include Melodies of Empowerment at The Nehru Centre in London, a workshop on ADHD in women and girls, and an evening at the House of Commons.
